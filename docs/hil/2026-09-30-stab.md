## Manual channel plan

### Found and fixed on the bench

- An offer after an offer that was never released was refused with
  `Conflict` (the host reused the epoch; the gateway ledger had already issued
  it). Fixed on the host: the next epoch passes both the active epoch and the
  ledger sequence.
- A member that had caught up to a commit whose plan had expired stayed in
  recovery (the switch time and validity were long past) and then refused every
  new plan for its 10-minute cooldown. Fixed: a verified commit whose channel
  the radio already follows is recorded as applied without a timed switch.
- A member that restarted between READY and the commit waited for the blob to
  be refetched although it had stored that blob itself; on hardware the refetch
  did not complete and the member kept booting on the old channel (the relay
  bound its gateway only 17–30 s after each reset). Fixed: a late commit uses
  the stored blob. After the fix the relay rejoined in 27.5 s and every later
  reboot started on the site's channel (10/10 in the relay reset run).
- The stranded search first waited out a clockless commit (the endpoint found
  the site only 137 s after boot) and then listened on a candidate without
  prompting discovery (bound 44 s later). Fixed: the search runs while a commit
  waits for a clock, and a searched candidate re-arms rediscovery.

### Channel 1 at this bench

The relay-only 6 → 1 switch delivered 20/20 at 13:06 and 0/20 at 14:58 with the
same firmware. At 14:58 the relay logged all 20 messages; its acknowledgements
did not reach the gateway (`HOP_ACCEPT_TIMEOUT`, `ADMISSION_NO_ACK_SLOT`,
`session replay` on the retries). Raw relay→gateway TX failures:

| Run | Channel | TX ok / failed | Failure |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| gateway reset `m06/gw4` (13:50) | 1 | 140 / 5 | 3.4 % |
| gateway reset `final/gw` (14:30) | 1 | 163 / 59 | 27 % |
| plan switch `final/plan-relay` (14:58) | 1 | 14 / 22 | 61 % |
| gateway reset `final/gw6` (15:20) | 6 | 142 / 6 | 4 % |

The same firmware switched 6 → 11 → 6 without loss. Channel 1 at this bench is
impaired and time-varying on the gateway's receive side; the plan acceptance
here used channel 11.

### Endpoint board fault

Right after the 3-board 6 → 1 switch at 12:09 (Node2 19/20, Node3 0/20) the
endpoint (`…47:54`) stopped receiving: 0–16 ESP-NOW frames per minute on any
channel while the relay heard about 30 per minute, across 5 EN resets, a
`MIGRATION=0` image that boots on channel 6 with no switch at all, and an image
with full RF calibration (`CONFIG_ESP_PHY_RF_CAL_FULL`). TX completions stay
normal. This needs a USB power cycle and a look at the board and antenna; it is
not a software state (no NVS or plan state survives the `MIGRATION=0` image's
boot on channel 6). The endpoint was revoked during the first J05 attempt and
is not a member at the end of the round.

A second plan offered to a gateway that had been up for a while was committed
by the gateway but never prepared by the relay twice (13:18 and 15:35); both
times a gateway reboot made the next offer READY in 5 s (follow-up G2).

## J05 over the recovery join

Relay as B (the endpoint had no working radio). The relay was held in the ROM
download mode while the host revoked it, so the gateway applied the new RRS1
and cancelled the removal notice before the relay could hear it. After the
release the relay's refresh join returned `Removed` 66 s after boot
(`membership removed … traffic stopped`), the removal journal ran its 600 s
holdoff and restarted the relay unassigned. That boot showed
`local_revocation: none` and `lifecycle: journaled`; the approver readmitted it
at generation 2 (`previously_removed: true`, same kid) and 10/10 sends
delivered. Before this branch the RLV1 Blocked record written by the recovery
join path stayed after the holdoff and refused every readmit (harness
reproduction `mesh_j05_revoke_learned_by_recovery_join_returns`).

## M06 gateway reset

`scripts/gw_cycles.py`: the daemon keeps running; each cycle pulses the C3 EN
line through its USB Serial/JTAG port, sends two probes during the USB outage,
waits for the new authenticated session and sends 10 messages 2 s apart to
Node2.

| Firmware | Channel | Delivered | Stalled cycles (first delivery) |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| this branch (`a859aacc`, `head/gw6`) | 6 | 187/200 | 1 (20.3 s) |
| this branch, A/B control (`ab-pre/gw6`) | 6 | 183/200 | 2 (18.4, 20.4 s) |
| handshake experiment, reverted (`final/gw6`) | 6 | 198/200 | 0 (max 4.3 s) |
| this branch, earlier build (`m06/gw*`) | 1 | 92/100, 100/100, 171/200, 189/200 | 1, 0, 3, 1 |
| handshake experiment (`final/gw`) | 1 | 146/200 | 4 (channel 1 impaired, above) |

Every cycle re-authenticated in 2.1–2.4 s, every accepted send reached a
terminal state, and every send issued before the new session was refused
(`authorization failed`, 40/40 per 20 cycles). The stalls come from the
end-to-end EDHOC after the reboot: the relay's single EDHOC flight is still
held by the finished link exchange (its m4 resend duty lasts the exchange's
8 s deadline), so the gateway's m1 is parked, the gateway gives up after its
retries and starts over behind the same flight (reproduced in the harness; the row is in the follow-up).
Releasing that flight when the same peer sends its next m1 fixed the harness
row and gave 198/200 on hardware, but it moved the timing of three existing
harness rows; the change was reverted from this branch and is follow-up G1.

## S7 simultaneous boot

Five `reset_all.py` runs of the three boards with the relay image that has
remote config and migration (`CONFIG=y`, `MIGRATION=2`): all routes 5.7, 4.9,
16.3, 9.4 and 4.6 s after the common reset; the relay held two neighbours in
every run by its first telemetry line (60 s) and its heap stayed at
73,728–75,776 B largest. The H1 stall (largest block 14 KB, no discovery) did
not recur. The H1 image also ran the HIL gateway sender; this one does not.
