### Channel 1 diagnosis

The final image's 6 → 1 release had both required members READY. All three
radios switched; both C6 logs report radio generation 1, Wi-Fi channel readback
1, committed channel 1, and no quarantine/fence. Within approximately 40 s after
cutover, Node2 and Node3 each delivered **0/20**. Raw C6 TX callbacks reported
high failure rates (relay 12 success / 23 failure; endpoint 12 / 29 in the
subsequent telemetry interval). After rebooting only the gateway onto its saved
channel-1 plan, USB reauthenticated in 2.03 s, routes did not return within 90 s,
and both members again delivered **0/20**.

A stale gateway cutover state alone does not explain this fresh-start failure.
The evidence does **not** establish whether bench RF, hardware, or a
channel-dependent driver/radio defect is responsible. The required 6 → 1 → 6
acceptance is not passed; a different channel is diagnostic evidence only.
The return offer could not release with isolated required members, as expected.
