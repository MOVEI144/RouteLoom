GPIO failures abort startup. C3 has no RF-switch calls. Four 30-second phases use LR250, 128-byte public markers, channels 6/1 and requested/read-back power 40/8 qdBm. **RF conclusion:** the setup is associated with improvement in several earlier C6 outgoing failures, particularly both C6-to-C6 directions on channel 1/40 qdBm. No spare-board/antenna/environment or isolated PHY control was available. SDK relay-reset failures also remain on channel 6, whose raw control was 120/120.
