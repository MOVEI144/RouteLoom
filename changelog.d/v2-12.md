### Changed

- **Breaking:** core C ABI 3 (`RL_ABI_VERSION` 3). Every public struct and
  vtable, including the radio, security and observer vtables, starts with
  `struct_size` and `version`; a short `struct_size` or another version is
  refused with `RL_STATUS_INVALID_ARGUMENT`. Fill headers with
  `rl_struct_init()` or the `rl_*_init()` helpers and rebuild.
- **Breaking:** `rl_node_config_init_full()`, `rl_reply_peer_vtable_init()`,
  `RL_NODE_CONFIG_SIZE_BASE`, `RL_NODE_CONFIG_SIZE_GATEWAY2` and
  `RL_REPLY_PEER_VERSION` are removed; `rl_node_config_init()` fills the whole
  struct. The C security vtable is documented as test/porting only (static
  keys); production security is the ESP-IDF Device API.

### Added

- `rl_abi_version()`, `rl_get_capabilities()` (`RL_CAP_*`) and
  `rl_next_deadline()`.
- APPLIED over C: `rl_send_applied()`, `rl_applied_lease()`,
  `rl_get_applied_result()`, and the asynchronous endpoint
  (`on_applied_request` ticket, `rl_complete_applied()`). The C++
  `AppliedEndpointSink` may defer its verdict the same way
  (`AppliedReply::deferred`, `MeshNode::complete_applied()`).
- `reason_id` in `rl_delivery_result_t` and `rl_group_result_t`, from the
  delivery reason codes now registered in `protocol/manifest.json`
  (`ROUTELOOM_REASON_*`, `ROUTELOOM_REASON_TABLE` in `version.h`).
- `protocol/abi-golden/core-abi3.json`: ILP32 and LP64 layouts, checked by
  `tools/abi_golden.py`.
