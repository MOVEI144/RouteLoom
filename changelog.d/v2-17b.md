Distribution bridge, relay and endpoint images default to MemberEdhoc
(Candidate). Quick-start and example builds keep DevRam (Development).
Member images exclude development PSK providers, and CI rejects development
keys or providers in the linked image. Runtime also reports Candidate for
Member security, preventing the Development-only receive-context fallback.
Release selection explicitly preserves both Member and DevRam images.
H2 hardware regression will run after all v2 PRs are merged; it is not a
merge prerequisite for this default change. Candidate is not Production.
DevRam keeps reporting Development after a radio failure enters recovery.
