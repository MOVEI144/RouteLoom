### Security

- **Breaking:** HostLink (RLU1) protocol 2. The USB session is authenticated
  with HKDF-SHA-256 and HMAC-SHA-256 instead of the development mixer: the
  key is derived from the gateway's hostlink secret, HELLO/AUTH tags and
  per-direction frame MACs (128-bit tags, 64-bit counters, a fresh replay
  window per session) bind the offered and selected version, carrier, Node,
  Boot and full 64-bit Network IDs, capabilities and the host principal.
  Protocol 1 hosts and devices are refused; there is no fallback. Update the
  firmware and routeloom-host together.
- HMAC-SHA-256 wipes its key pads and intermediate digests on every call.

### Added

- `routeloom-host --hostlink-credentials DIR`: per-gateway secrets in
  `DIR/<node:016x>.key` (0600, 1..63 printable ASCII), chosen by the NodeId
  the gateway reports. `--usb-dev-secret-file` stays as the development
  profile; the two options are mutually exclusive.
- The meshviz provisioning wizard writes a fresh secret per gateway to its
  rlkeys and to `<site>/hostlink/<node>.key`, and the site supervisor starts
  the daemon with `--hostlink-credentials` when that directory exists.

### Changed

- **Breaking:** USB Error and DeliveryEvent frames carry a u16 reason id
  (`reason_id || detail_len || detail`, the hostlink and delivery areas of
  `protocol/manifest.json`); only unregistered reasons still carry text.
  API1 reports the same reason strings as before.
- `protocol/usb-golden` is regenerated for protocol 2; the unused protocol 1
  `join-relay` vectors are removed (`join-relay-v2` covers the family).
