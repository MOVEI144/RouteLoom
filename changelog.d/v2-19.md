### Added

- Optional authenticated unicast AppObject transfer (default OFF), with explicit C/C++ Device APIs and API1 `objects.submit/get/cancel`. Objects up to 4096 bytes use 121-byte chunks, a two-frame window, bounded receive/completion records and Bulk pacing; ordinary sends keep their 128-byte limit.
- HostLink object upload and verified `objects` subscriptions, wire golden vectors, parser fuzzing and real-Owner transfer scenarios. Existing config reassembly uses the shared non-owning assembler without changing its wire format.
