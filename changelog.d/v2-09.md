### Changed

- `reference_node` and `bridge_node` images build with `-Os`
  (`CONFIG_COMPILER_OPTIMIZATION_SIZE=y`): on ESP32-C3 the app is about 11%
  smaller and about 8 KiB more static RAM stays free. For a debug build append
  `CONFIG_COMPILER_OPTIMIZATION_DEBUG=y` (the `*-debug_og` firmware cells).
- The Owner mesh E2E worlds power every node on at the same tick. The former
  `[0, 2000, 12000]` boot offsets remain the compatibility plan of the
  route-loss rows.

### Fixed

- A member whose radio was isolated long enough to start re-verifying its
  retained site reattaches without a reset once the radio heals: while out of
  range it rescans at least every 15 s, a re-verification that never heard its
  site no longer blocks the next one for 10 minutes, and the re-adopted member
  restarts neighbor repair at once.
- Two members that answered each other's discovery no longer both wait for the
  other's first handshake message: the lower NodeId initiates. A late frame of
  a superseded exchange can no longer take over the new exchange's leg.
- The single link handshake chunk slot is freed after every chunked message.
  It could stay held and refuse the chunks of every later link handshake.
- The Site Authority keeps a member's durable staged or active group-key
  acknowledgement when the resends of a repair round go unanswered; the member
  is no longer reported as unknown.
- A member with a live neighbor but no route to its gateway seeks another peer
  after route advertisements have had time to recover. Simultaneously rebooted
  diamond meshes can reconnect without a new join or a rescue reset.
- After a committed group-key activation, the Owner advances the pairwise
  session bank's epoch. Members that missed two rotations can establish fresh
  sessions, while sessions past their group-key lifetime are retired.
